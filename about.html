The Wallflower Story

Boutique Luxury with a Soul

Kota is celebrated across India as a city of diligence and ambition. Every month, thousands of hopeful students, caring parents, corporate executives, and cultural explorers arrive in Kota. Yet, for years, the city lacked a true boutique hotel that offered both acoustic peace and contemporary luxury.

Hotel Wallflower was born to fill that void. Named after the quiet elegance of timeless hospitality, Wallflower offers sanctuary from the city's fast tempo. Behind our contemporary glass facade lies a world of soundproofed suites, velvet lounge armchairs, pure and wholesome culinary delights, and warm Rajasthani smiles.
