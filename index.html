ABOUT THE HOTEL

A sanctuary crafted for peace, elegance, and deep rest.

Nestled along Kota’s prominent Jhalawar Road, The Wallflower Hotel redefines boutique hospitality with an uncompromising focus on serenity. Here, sculpted stone, handcrafted teak, and acoustic isolation converge to offer visitors a calm retreat from the city’s restless rhythm.
