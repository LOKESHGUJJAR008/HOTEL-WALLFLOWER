Tailored For Kota Travelers

Designed for Your Total Comfort

Whether you are visiting for coaching admissions, corporate meetings, or city exploration, our curated boutique amenities cater to every requirement.

High-Speed Free WiFi

Dedicated 100+ Mbps high-speed fiber internet coverage across all guest rooms, lobby, and dining areas. Perfect for online coaching lectures and video conferences.

Free Valet & Parking

Complimentary secure parking inside our premises with round-the-clock CCTV surveillance, dedicated security guards, and professional valet service.

24x7 Front Desk

Our concierge team is at your service 24 hours a day for late night arrivals, local coaching travel guidance, wake-up calls, and luggage storage.

In-Room Dining Service

Savor hot, wholesome meals from The Wallflower Bistro in the privacy of your room. Available 24 hours a day with prompt, courteous service.

Modern Fitness Centre

Equipped with state-of-the-art treadmills, cross-trainers, multi-station strength machines, free dumbbells, and clean workout towels with garden views.

Pool Table & Lounge

Unwind in our signature games lounge featuring a regulation green felt billiards table, Chesterfield sofas, chess sets, and a cozy evening ambiance.

Car Rental & Transfers

Effortless chauffeur-driven airport transfers, railway station pick-ups (Kota Junction), and dedicated cabs for visiting Allen, Resonance, or tourist sites.

Complimentary Breakfast

Wake up to a lavish buffet of hot Rajasthani poha, parathas, idlis, seasonal fruits, live egg stations, and artisanal coffee included with direct bookings.

Meeting & Banquet Facility

Well-appointed boardroom and banquet hall equipped with ultra HD projection, wireless mics, high-speed WiFi, and customized corporate catering.

Daily Sanitization & Laundry

Spotless hygiene with hospital-grade sanitization, daily fresh linen changes, evening turndown service, and same-day express laundry/dry cleaning.

Recreation Spotlight

The Billiards & Games Lounge

Designed as an exclusive retreat where parents can unwind while waiting, or colleagues can brainstorm over a friendly game of pool. Enjoy leather Chesterfields, warm wood accents, and beverage service directly to the lounge table.
