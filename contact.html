Frequently Asked

Frequently Asked Questions

What are the Check-in and Check-out timings?

Standard check-in is 01:00 PM and check-out is 11:00 AM. Early check-in and late check-out can be accommodated based on room availability at no extra charge if booked directly.

Is Hotel Wallflower suitable for students and visiting parents?

Yes! We are especially favored by students and parents. Our Superior Twin Rooms feature two dedicated study desks, sound-insulated windows, 100 Mbps fiber WiFi, and pure, wholesome home-style food at our Bistro.

How far is the hotel from Kota Junction Railway Station?

Kota Junction is approximately 6.5 km (~15 minutes drive) away. We can arrange a comfortable private cab transfer for you with direct platform pickup.

Is free parking available for guests?

Yes, we offer complimentary, secure parking inside our premises with 24-hour CCTV surveillance and valet assistance.
